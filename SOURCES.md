## 1. Ukraine — Kerch Bridge road arch brought down
Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to
occupied Crimea on Tuesday; the road arch collapsed into the Kerch Strait
and road traffic was halted. Russia's Defence Ministry said it shot down
1,441 Ukrainian drones overnight.

## 2. Middle East — U.S. strikes Qeshm Island; tankers hit mines
U.S. fighter jets struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the
Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday. Iran said three oil tankers struck naval mines
while crossing the strait, and reported a drone attack on a U.S. radar
system at an air base in Bahrain.

## 3. Asia — Russian jets breach Japan's airspace
Two suspected Russian aircraft entered Japanese airspace off Fukuoka
Prefecture shortly after 4 a.m. on Tuesday; Japan scrambled fighter jets
and lodged a protest. The first such violation since September 2024.
South Korea also scrambled jets.

## 4. Climate — COP31 draft urges faster fossil-fuel exit
Ministers took charge of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya on Tuesday and
began responding to the first draft of a possible final deal, which calls
for accelerated action to phase out fossil fuels and to triple adaptation
finance to at least $120 billion a year by 2035.

## 5. Gaza — West presses Israel to open aid routes
